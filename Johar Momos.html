About Johar Momos

Johar Momos is your local spot for fresh, piping-hot momos. We've got every flavor you could crave—Veg, Soya, Paneer, Kurkure, Afghani, and many more items—all at super affordable prices.

Whether you're in the mood for a light steamed plate, something crisp, or something creamy, pick what hits the spot.

Check out our special combos, or even build your own custom combo just the way you like it. Come by for a quick bite with friends, or call ahead for your order!
